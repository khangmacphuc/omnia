# Roadmaps, blueprints and plans

For software, research, learning, science, documents and automation, Omnia can
materialize:

- objective and non-goals;
- requirements and constraints;
- architecture/blueprint;
- phases and milestones;
- task DAG and critical path;
- owners, budgets and deadlines;
- risks and assumptions;
- sources and evidence;
- acceptance criteria;
- artifacts and verification;
- calendar, Gantt, board, graph and Markdown views.

## Learning/exam plans

Include exam date, syllabus, diagnostic, prerequisite graph, topic taxonomy,
problem types, recognition cues, general solution methods, proof dependencies,
weekly/daily schedule, spaced repetition, active recall, error notebook,
mock exams, progress, weakness map and adaptive rescheduling.

## Research plans

Include question, hypothesis, literature map, source strategy, evidence matrix,
method, experiment, data, analysis, limitations, gap, reproducibility and
publication artifacts.

## Smart updates

Update only after material events such as requirement correction, test failure,
new source, milestone completion, architecture decision or deadline change.
Preserve user-authored sections, show diff and explain why the plan changed.
