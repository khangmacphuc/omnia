# Multimodal bridge and specialist personas

Omnia must make a selected model useful across images, audio, video, PDF,
Office, archives, code, data and scientific artifacts even when that model
lacks a native modality. A capability planner chooses direct provider input,
local transform, approved specialist model, hybrid ensemble, grounded surrogate
or human review and preserves every original/derivative/source reference,
confidence, cost, privacy decision and modality-specific fidelity result.

`Auto` is only a requested routing preference: the durable plan must record the
resolved non-auto route, rejected alternatives, frozen provider capability and
policy snapshots, data-boundary/egress decision, untrusted-content treatment,
source coordinates, loss/degradation disclosure and validator evidence. File
content is untrusted data, never an instruction that can grant tools, network,
memory, secrets or execution authority. A route without sufficient fidelity or
current data-egress authority must fail closed to review or an exact blocked
reason rather than silently changing provider or inventing a source-equivalent
answer.

Omnia also ships a versioned built-in catalog of specialist agents/personas for
orchestration, UI/UX/icon/motion/accessibility, research/learning, competitive
programming, mathematics, physics/science, software/code, defensive cyber,
AI/ML/DS, engineering, documents/media, providers/runtimes, security, testing,
performance and release/evidence work. Roles have explicit model/tool/context/
authority/evaluation contracts and can form review pairs, panels, teams,
debates, swarms or custom DAGs.

Completion requires typed bridge and role schemas, native capability/authority
checks, inspectable routing, local/provider fallbacks, modality quality gates,
provenance, accessible UI, representative evaluations, no hidden privilege,
untrusted-attachment containment, explicit provider-boundary records and
truthful `ready` versus `ready-with-degradation` outcomes.

The bridge execution plane must use isolated local transform workers, typed
canonical intermediate representations, source-coordinate closure, durable
stage receipts and `outcome-unknown` reconciliation. Academic and technical
teams compile typed work units for learning, research, competitive programming,
mathematics, physics and natural science, production code, AI/data, defensive
cyber, engineering, communication and artifact creation instead of resolving
the request to one generic role label.
